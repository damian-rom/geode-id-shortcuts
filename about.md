# ID Shortcuts

Turns IDs written in comments, messages and descriptions into **clickable shortcuts**.

Someone recommends a level or list by pasting its ID, and instead of memorising the number, backing
out and typing it into search, you just **Ctrl + Click** it (or just **tap** it on mobile devices)
and land straight on the level or list page.

## Settings

- **Enable ID shortcuts** - turn the whole thing off, without restarting the game.
- **Shortcut color** - pick the highlight color.
- **Treat IDs as** - always open levels, always open lists, or let you decide each time.
- **Require Ctrl to open** - turn off if you would rather a plain click opened the level.
- **Minimum digits** - how many digits a number needs for it to count as an ID.
- **Debug mode** - shows the clickable areas and writes detailed logs.

## Notes

Not every number is an ID, so the mod skips ones that clearly aren't, like dates, times, percentages, thousand separators, and counts like "12345 attempts" or "Demon #1234".
It will still occasionally highlight a number that isn't a level, but don't worry. Nothing happens
unless you Ctrl + Click it, and non-existent IDs just won't open anything.
